Jigripper

PRIVACY POLICY · Last updated 14 June 2026

No data collected · Everything runs on your device

The short version

Jigripper is an Irish traditional-music tune identifier. It listens to live music through your device's microphone and tells you what tune is being played, following along on the sheet music. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data — full stop. All processing happens on your device.

Microphone

Jigripper uses the microphone only while you are actively using the listen feature, to identify the music in real time. The audio is analysed on-device, moment to moment, and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Nothing leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. Jigripper has no accounts, no sign-in, and no personal information of any kind. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDK that collects data.

On-device processing

Tune identification, the live score-following cursor, and instrument detection all run locally using neural networks bundled inside the app. Core features work fully offline — no internet connection is required, and no audio or usage data is uploaded.

What's stored on your device

Your settings and any tunes you save or favourite are kept locally on your device only. Removing the app removes them. They are never synced to us or to any server.

Children

Because Jigripper collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
