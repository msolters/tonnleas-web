JigRipper Mini

Name that tune, on your own site

A listen ring the size of a search box. Your visitors tap it, play a few bars of an Irish traditional tune, and it tells them which one it is, with a link to the tune on The Session. It is the JigRipper app, in miniature.

Try it

Two made-up websites, each with JigRipper Mini placed in it using the two lines of code below.

yourtunesite.example

Tuesday night session

Upstairs at the pub · from 9 o'clock

Heard a tune you didn't know? Tap the ring and play it back.

a-darker-site.example

Learn a tune a week

The widget takes its colours from the page it sits on.

Add it to your site

It's free, under our terms of use. Put this where you want the widget to appear:

<div class="jigripper-mini"></div>
<script src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed.js" async></script>
  • Use the div as many times as you like; one script tag serves them all.
  • It picks light or dark from your page. To choose, add data-theme="light" or data-theme="dark" to the div.
  • "Open on The Session" opens the tune in a new tab, so your visitors keep your page. To open it in the same tab instead, add data-session-links="same-tab" to the div ("new-tab" is the default).
  • It is up to 536 pixels wide and 92 tall, and narrows to fit a phone.
  • Adding the element after the page has loaded, as single-page apps do? Call JigRipperMini.mount() once it is in the page. JigRipperMini.open() opens a widget as if it had been tapped.

For example, a dark widget whose tune links open in the same tab:

<div class="jigripper-mini"
     data-theme="dark"
     data-session-links="same-tab"></div>
<script src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed.js" async></script>

If your site's security settings don't allow scripts from other sites, use this one tag instead:

<iframe src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed/" allow="microphone; autoplay"
        title="JigRipper Mini" loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%;max-width:536px;height:92px;border:0;color-scheme:light"></iframe>

The same options go on the end of the iframe's address: ?theme=light for the light style, ?links=same to open tune links in the same tab, or both: ?theme=light&links=same.

What your visitors get

  • Almost nothing loads until they tap. Before that the widget is a small card from jigripper.com, a few kilobytes. JigRipper itself waits for the tap.
  • One tap. It downloads JigRipper's tune model once, about 130 MB, which the browser keeps, then starts listening. A few browsers ask for one more tap on the ring first.
  • Private. What they play is heard on their own device. It is never recorded and never sent anywhere, and the widget sets no cookies on your site.
  • The real thing. The same tune recognition as the JigRipper app, which knows over 22,000 tunes.
