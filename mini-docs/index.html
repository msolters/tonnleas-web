JigRipper Mini

Name that tune, on your own site

A listen ring the size of a search box. Your visitors tap it, play a few bars of an Irish traditional tune, and it tells them which one it is, with a link to the tune on The Session. It is the JigRipper app, in miniature.

Try it

Two made-up websites, each with JigRipper Mini placed in it using the two lines of code below.

Heard a tune you didn't know? Tap the ring and play it back.

Add it to your site

It's free, under our terms of use. There are two ways to add it:

  • The script, recommended: an element and a script tag. It matches your page's colours and text size by itself.
  • An iframe: one tag and no script, for sites whose security settings block scripts from other sites. You choose its colours yourself.

With the script

Put this where you want the widget to appear:

<div class="jigripper-mini"></div>
<script src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed.js" async></script>

Use the div as many times as you like; one script tag serves them all.

Options go on the div:

data-theme="light" or data-theme="dark"
Light or dark colours. Leave it out and the widget picks from your page's background.
data-session-links="same-tab" or data-session-links="new-tab"
Where "Open on The Session" opens the tune. The default is a new tab, so your visitors keep your page.
data-guesses="on"
Under the widget, the top 10 guesses, each with how sure it is, the surest at the top. Each opens on The Session, so your visitors can check every tune it considered. The list holds still when the playing stops. The widget is then 333 pixels tall instead of 92. Off by default.

For example, a dark widget whose tune links open in the same tab:

<div class="jigripper-mini"
     data-theme="dark"
     data-session-links="same-tab"></div>
<script src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed.js" async></script>

Size takes care of itself. The widget fills the width of the element it is in, up to 536 pixels, and narrows to fit a phone; it is 92 pixels tall, or 333 with the guesses. Where your text is set larger than 16 pixels, it grows with it, up to half as large again.

Pages that add the element later, as single-page apps do, call JigRipperMini.mount() once it is in the page, or JigRipperMini.mount(element) for one element. JigRipperMini.open() opens a widget as if it had been tapped.

With an iframe

Put this where you want the widget to appear:

<iframe src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed/?theme=light"
        allow="microphone; autoplay" title="JigRipper Mini" loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%; max-width:clamp(536px,33.5rem,804px);
               height:clamp(92px,5.75rem,138px); border:0; color-scheme:light"></iframe>

Options go on the end of the address: ? before the first, & between them.

theme=light or theme=dark
Light or dark colours. An iframe can't see the page around it, so set this to match yours. Leave it out and the widget is dark.
links=same
"Open on The Session" opens the tune in the same tab. Leave it out for a new tab, so your visitors keep your page.
guesses=1
The top 10 guesses under the widget, as with the script. Make the iframe taller to fit: height:clamp(333px,20.8125rem,499.5px).

For example, a dark widget whose tune links open in the same tab:

<iframe src="https://www.jigripper.com/mini/embed/?theme=dark&links=same"
        allow="microphone; autoplay" title="JigRipper Mini" loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%; max-width:clamp(536px,33.5rem,804px);
               height:clamp(92px,5.75rem,138px); border:0; color-scheme:light"></iframe>

The rest of the tag:

allow="microphone; autoplay"
Required. Without it the widget can't listen.
title
Names the widget for screen readers.
loading="lazy"
Optional. The card loads when it nears the screen.
width, max-width and height
The size, the same as the script's: 536 × 92 pixels at 16-pixel text (536 × 333 with guesses=1). Set in rem, it grows with your page's text where that is set larger, up to half as large again.
border:0
No frame; the widget draws its own edge.
color-scheme:light
Keep it, whichever theme you choose. It doesn't set the widget's colours (that is theme, in the address); it keeps the frame's background clear, where some browsers would otherwise paint a solid box behind the widget.

JigRipperMini.mount() and open() come with the script, so they don't apply to an iframe.

What your visitors get

  • Almost nothing loads until they tap. Before that the widget is a small card from jigripper.com, a few kilobytes. JigRipper itself waits for the tap.
  • One tap. It downloads JigRipper's tune model once, about 130 MB, which the browser keeps, then starts listening. A few browsers ask for one more tap on the ring first.
  • Private. What they play is heard on their own device. It is never recorded and never sent anywhere, and the widget sets no cookies on your site. Like every page on jigripper.com, it counts visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies (privacy policy).
  • The real thing. The same tune recognition as the JigRipper app, which knows over 22,000 tunes.
