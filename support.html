Jigripper

SUPPORT

Jigripper listens to live Irish traditional music and identifies the tune being played — then follows along on the sheet music with a moving cursor. It runs entirely on your device.

Getting started

Open the app and tap the centre ring to start listening. Play (or hold the phone near) some Irish trad, and Jigripper will name the tune and show the notation. Turn on the cursor (⌖) to have it follow along as the tune plays.

Common questions

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Identification, the score-following cursor, and instrument detection all run on-device. Jigripper works fully offline.

What music does it recognise?

Irish traditional tunes — reels, jigs, hornpipes, polkas and more — drawn from a large session repertoire of tens of thousands of tunes.

Is my audio private?

Yes. The microphone is used only while you're listening, and the audio is never recorded or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

Why is the app a large download?

It bundles a full neural-network model covering tens of thousands of tunes so identification works offline, with nothing to download or stream later.

The cursor or ID seems off — what helps?

A clear, reasonably loud source and a quiet room help most. Re-miked audio (a tune played back through a speaker) is the hardest case. On iOS, make sure Low Power Mode is off, as it can slow the on-device analysis.
