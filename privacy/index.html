The short version

JigRipper listens to traditional music and names the tune. All of that happens on your device. We don't collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no trackers.

Microphone audio

JigRipper requests microphone access solely to identify tunes being played around you. Audio is analyzed in real time, entirely on your device, and is never recorded to permanent storage, never uploaded, and never shared. When you stop listening, the audio is gone.

If you use the "analyze a recording" feature, the file you choose is likewise processed entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your saved tunes, sets, session history, search history, and settings are stored locally on your device only. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None. JigRipper has no server component of its own, no user accounts, and no telemetry. We cannot see how you use the app — by design.

The web version at jigripper.com is a static site; its hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare) may keep ordinary, transient web server logs as any website's host does. We do not use cookies or run any analytics on it.

Children

JigRipper is suitable for all ages and, collecting no data at all, collects no data from children.

Tune database — ODbL attribution & offer

JigRipper's tune metadata (names, types, keys, settings) is derived from the community database of The Session, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0.

  • Source data: github.com/adactio/TheSession-data
  • Our derived database (the tune index shipped in the app) is publicly served on this site under the same ODbL v1.0 terms, and is also available on request — email the address below and we'll send it to you.
